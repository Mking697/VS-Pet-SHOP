Frequently Asked Questions

Are your pets healthy and vaccinated?

Yes. Every pet is health-checked and given its age-appropriate vaccinations before rehoming. You receive the vaccination card and a schedule for the remaining doses.

Do you deliver pet food and accessories?

We deliver locally, usually the same day for orders placed before 6 PM. Just send us your order on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the timing.

Can you help me pick the right breed?

Absolutely — that's one of our favourite parts of the job. Tell us about your home size, family, working hours and budget, and we'll recommend what genuinely fits.

What are your opening hours?

We're open 24 hours a day, all seven days. Whether it's a late-night food run or an early morning grooming slot, you'll find us open.

Do you offer grooming services in-store?

Yes — grooming is one of our regular services for dogs and cats: bath, trim, nail clipping and coat care. Many customers bring their pets in weekly. Call or WhatsApp to book a slot.

Where exactly are you located?

Shop No. 39, Habitech Panchtatva, near Galaxy Vega, Tech Zone IV, Amrapali Dream Valley, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201318. Tap any "Get Directions" button on this page and Google Maps will take you straight there.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash, UPI, and all major debit and credit cards.
