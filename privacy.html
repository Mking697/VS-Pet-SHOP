Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you visit vspetshop.com, the website of V S Pet Shop, Greater Noida. We have tried to write it plainly rather than in legal language.

The short version

This website has no server-side database and no login. It does not store your name, phone number, or message anywhere on this site. When you use the enquiry form, nothing is submitted to us — the form simply opens WhatsApp on your device with a message already typed out, which you then choose to send or not.

Information you choose to give us

  • Enquiry form. The name, phone number, interest and message you type are used only to build a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is transmitted until you press send in WhatsApp. From that point the conversation is governed by WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • Calls and WhatsApp messages. If you call or message the shop, we will have your phone number and whatever you tell us. We use it to answer your enquiry and, where relevant, to fulfil an order or a grooming appointment. We do not sell it or pass it to anyone else.

Information collected automatically

We use analytics and advertising tools to understand how people find and use the site. Depending on what is switched on at any given time, these may include:

  • Google Analytics — page views, how you arrived, roughly where you are (city level), and which buttons you use. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Ads — whether a visit that came from one of our adverts led to a call, a WhatsApp message or an enquiry, so we can tell which adverts are worth running.
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram ads) — the same, for adverts we run on Facebook and Instagram. See Meta's privacy policy.

These tools set cookies and may record your IP address. They tell us about patterns across visitors; they do not tell us who you personally are.

Other services this page loads

  • Google Maps — the map in the contact section is embedded from Google and loads only when you scroll to it.
  • Unsplash — some photographs on the site are currently served from Unsplash's image service.
  • Hostinger — our web host, which keeps standard server logs.

Stored on your own device

If you tap the heart icon on a category to save it as a favourite, that choice is kept in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it next time. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out add-on.
  • You can adjust what adverts you see in your Google Ad Centre and in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings.
  • You can ask us to delete any message history you have with us — just ask on WhatsApp or by email.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and the date at the top of it.
